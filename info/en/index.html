FAQ

What you’re probably wondering.

Do I need to already have a course? +

No. We work with you from the idea. You just need expertise or a method that gets results.

What kind of knowledge can I turn into a product? +

Business, marketing, fitness, cooking, languages, finance, design, music, real estate, relationships… If you know something others want to learn, there’s a market.

How much time do I need to invest? +

On average 8 to 12 hours spread over 3 to 4 weeks. We do the rest.

How long does the project take? +

A full project, 4 to 8 weeks. A masterclass or ebook, 2 to 3 weeks.

How much can I earn? +

It depends on your topic, audience and price. A $1,500 MXN course selling 100 units a month generates $150,000 MXN. On the call we’ll tell you the realistic potential for your case.
