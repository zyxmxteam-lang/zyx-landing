FAQ

What people ask before getting started.

Is the Meta ad budget included? +

No. The $10,000 covers the full service. Ad spend is separate and you control it from your own account. We recommend at least $5,000 MXN per month.

Do I have to record the videos? +

Yes, and that’s what makes them work: your face, your voice, your personality. You record on your phone following the brief and we handle everything else. If you’d rather not be on camera, ask us about the AI Avatar.

What is the AI Avatar? +

A hyper-realistic avatar with your image, voice and style. You send us the script and the avatar records it for you, in any language. It’s an add-on service.

How much of my time does it take each month? +

About 2 hours: 30 minutes of strategy and 1 to 1.5 hours to record the 4 raw videos.

What kind of business is it for? +

Info products, coaching, consulting, agencies, memberships: any business where you are the brand. It works less well for pure physical-product ecommerce.

I already tried Meta Ads and it didn’t work. +

Most campaigns fail because of the creative, not the targeting. We start with the video: 40 seconds that earn attention before they sell.

When will I see results? +

Meta optimizes in 1 to 3 weeks. The first data comes in month 1; consistent growth in months 2 and 3.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. No annual contracts or penalties. Just give 7 days’ notice.
