FAQ

What people ask about AI ads.

Can you tell it’s AI? +

With the current quality of video models, in short social formats it’s very hard to tell apart from a real shoot. We also follow each platform’s policies on AI-generated content.

Do I have to record anything? +

No. If you want to appear yourself, that’s our AI Clone service, trained from a single recording of you.

What do you need from me? +

Your offer, who you sell to and, if it’s a physical product, clear photos of it. We handle the script, generation, voice and editing.

What formats do you deliver? +

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories, with captions and music. Also 1:1 and 16:9 if you need them.

How much does it cost? +

It depends on the volume of videos and formats per month. Message us and we’ll send a quote based on your goal.
