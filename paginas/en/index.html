FAQ

What people ask before getting started.

What do I need to get started? +

A 30-minute call and basic info: what you sell, who you sell to and what you want the page to achieve. You don’t need to write anything.

What platform do you build on? +

HTML/CSS (static, ultra fast), WordPress or Webflow. We recommend whatever fits you best.

Is hosting included? +

Hosting and domain are separate, but we guide you (many options start at $0 the first year) and help connect your domain.

Can I ask for changes later? +

It includes 1 full round of revisions. Additional changes are quoted by scope.

I already have a page. Can you improve it? +

Yes. We redesign, rewrite or rebuild from scratch, depending on what the audit shows.

What kind of business is it for? +

Coaches, consultants, agencies, service businesses, info products, local businesses and personal brands.

What if I’m not happy with it? +

The revision round exists so you love your page. We work with you until you’re happy.
